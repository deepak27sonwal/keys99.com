Rajendra Nagar is in south-west Hyderabad, between Attapur, the Outer Ring Road and the airport side of the city. It is known for its large green campuses, including the state agricultural university and the National Institute of Rural Development, and for having more open space than most of the city.

Connectivity

  • Into the city: the PV Narasimha Rao Expressway links the Attapur side with Mehdipatnam and central Hyderabad.
  • Airport: Rajiv Gandhi International Airport at Shamshabad lies to the south.
  • IT jobs: the Outer Ring Road connects to Gachibowli and the Financial District.
  • Nearby: Attapur, Himayat Sagar and Shamshabad.

Living here

Attapur and Mehdipatnam nearby offer established markets, schools and hospitals. The large institutional campuses and the lakes nearby keep much of the area green and less crowded than the city centre.

Who it suits

Families who want a greener area with easy access to the airport, and professionals who commute to Gachibowli or the Financial District along the Outer Ring Road.
