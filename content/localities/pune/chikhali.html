Chikhali is a residential area in the north of Pimpri-Chinchwad, between Nigdi, Moshi and Talawade. It has grown quickly as Nigdi and Akurdi filled up, and is now mostly new apartment projects, with prices generally below those in the older Pradhikaran neighbourhoods next door.

Connectivity

  • Within PCMC: the Spine Road connects Chikhali with Moshi on one side and Nigdi and the Mumbai–Pune highway on the other.
  • Jobs: the Talawade IT park is next door, and Bhosari MIDC is a short drive east.
  • Rail: Akurdi and Nigdi are the nearest areas for suburban train stations on the Pune–Lonavala line.
  • Neighbours: Moshi to the east and Ravet to the south-west.

Living here

Chikhali is part of the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC). Nigdi and Akurdi nearby offer established schools, colleges, hospitals and markets, while shops and daily services are steadily opening within Chikhali itself. Some older roads are narrow, so visit a project at peak hours before booking.

Who it suits

Buyers who want to be close to Nigdi and Akurdi's schools and services at a lower price, and people working in Talawade or Bhosari.
