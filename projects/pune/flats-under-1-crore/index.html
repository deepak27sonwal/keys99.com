How much down payment do I need for a ₹ 1 Crore flat?

RBI rules let banks lend up to 90% of a home's value for loans up to ₹ 30 Lakh, 80% for loans up to ₹ 75 Lakh and 75% above that. For a ₹ 1 Crore flat, plan for a down payment of at least 25% (₹ 25 Lakh), plus stamp duty and registration charges, which banks do not fund.
