Living in Moshi, Pune

Moshi lies on the Pune–Nashik highway in the north of Pimpri-Chinchwad, between Bhosari and Chakan. Its position between two of the region's biggest industrial areas has made it one of Pimpri-Chinchwad's busiest growth corridors, with new residential projects alongside planned Pradhikaran sectors.

Connectivity

  • Highway: the Pune–Nashik highway runs through Moshi, connecting it to Bhosari and Pune in one direction and Chakan in the other.
  • Within PCMC: the Spine Road links Moshi with Chikhali, Nigdi and the Mumbai–Pune highway.
  • Jobs: Bhosari MIDC is next door, and the Chakan automobile and manufacturing belt is a short drive up the highway.
  • Neighbours: Charholi to the east and Chikhali to the west.

Living here

Moshi is part of the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC). Shops, schools and clinics have grown along the highway and inside the Pradhikaran sectors, and Bhosari and Pimpri are close for larger hospitals and markets. Traffic on the highway is heavy at peak hours, so check how far a project sits from it.

Who it suits

People working in Bhosari or Chakan who want a short commute, first-time buyers looking for value in Pimpri-Chinchwad, and investors betting on the industrial corridor's growth.
