Living in Charholi, Pune

Charholi (Charholi Budruk) is a fast-growing residential area on the north-eastern edge of Pimpri-Chinchwad, between Dighi, Moshi and the temple town of Alandi. Much of it was farmland until recently, so most homes here are in new gated projects and townships, and land prices are still lower than in the older parts of Pimpri-Chinchwad.

Connectivity

  • Pune city: Alandi Road runs south through Dighi and Vishrantwadi towards the city.
  • Airport: Pune airport at Lohegaon is reached through Dhanori, which makes Charholi one of the nearer Pimpri-Chinchwad localities for frequent flyers.
  • Jobs: the Bhosari MIDC industrial area is close by, and the Chakan industrial belt is reached along the Pune–Nashik highway through Moshi.
  • Planned: PMRDA's proposed Pune Ring Road is planned to pass through this belt; check its current status before treating it as a reason to buy.

Living here

Charholi is part of the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC). Daily shopping, schools and clinics are spread along Alandi Road and in neighbouring Dighi and Bhosari, while larger hospitals and malls are a drive away in Pimpri-Chinchwad or Vishrantwadi. The Indrayani river and Alandi give the area a quieter, greener feel than the city.

Who it suits

First-time buyers looking for a larger flat for their budget, families who want a newer gated community, and people working in Bhosari, Chakan or near the airport. Buyers who need a short daily commute to Hinjewadi or central Pune should compare it with Ravet or other Pune localities.
