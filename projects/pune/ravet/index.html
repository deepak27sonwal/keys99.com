Living in Ravet, Pune

Ravet sits on the western edge of Pimpri-Chinchwad, where the Mumbai–Pune Expressway meets the Mumbai–Bengaluru highway. Being at this junction, and close to Hinjewadi, has made it one of Pimpri-Chinchwad's most sought-after areas for new apartments, alongside neighbouring Punawale and Kiwale.

Connectivity

  • Highways: direct access to the Mumbai–Pune Expressway and the Mumbai–Bengaluru highway (the Pune bypass), so both Mumbai and southern Pune are easy to reach by road.
  • IT jobs: Hinjewadi's IT parks are reached through Punawale, Tathawade and Wakad.
  • Rail: Akurdi station, on the Pune–Lonavala suburban line, is close by.
  • Neighbours: Nigdi and Akurdi to the north-east, Chikhali further north.

Living here

Ravet is part of the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC). Akurdi and Nigdi nearby have well-known colleges, schools, hospitals and markets, and newer shops and restaurants have opened around Ravet and Punawale. The Pavana river runs along the area. Traffic near the highway junctions builds up at peak hours.

Who it suits

Professionals working in Hinjewadi or Wakad, people who travel to Mumbai often, and families who want newer projects close to Akurdi and Nigdi's schools and colleges.
