About Kohinoor Royal Tower

Kohinoor Group’s Kohinoor Royale Towers is a premium residential project positioned within/around Blue Ridge Township, Hinjewadi Phase 1, in West Pune. The developer's official project page lists 2 & 3 BHK homes, approximately 768–1,083 sq ft carpet area, across 2 towers on about 3 acres.

Kohinoor Royal Tower Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Prime Hinjewadi Phase 1 location — close to Pune's major IT/ITeS employment hub.
  • Strong township ecosystem — proximity to the Blue Ridge development gives access to established amenities and infrastructure.

Cons

  • Long wait for possession — the project is under construction, with the RERA timeline around 2030–31. That means your capital can remain locked for several years
  • Hinjewadi traffic — peak-hour congestion is repeatedly mentioned as a major drawback of the locality. This can matter significantly if you commute daily.
