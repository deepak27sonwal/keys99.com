About Keys99

This page is a working draft. Every highlighted field must be completed with real details before Keys99 goes live.

Keys99 helps home buyers find new residential projects in Pune and get in touch with the people selling them. Every project has its own page with its configurations, carpet areas, prices, floor plans, amenities, location and RERA registration, so you can compare projects side by side before you visit.

What we do

  • List new projects across Pune's growing localities, including Moshi, Charholi, Ravet and Chikhali, with the details buyers ask about first.
  • Show RERA details on every project page, so you can check a project's registration on the MahaRERA website yourself.
  • Connect you with the project when you send an enquiry or schedule a site visit, so you hear back from someone who can answer your questions.
